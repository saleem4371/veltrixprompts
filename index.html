FAQ

Do I need any technical skills?+
None at all. If you can type a message, you can use these prompts. No AI knowledge or technical background required. Everything is ready to copy and paste.
Which AI tools do the prompts work with?+
All major AI tools — ChatGPT (free or Plus), Claude, Google Gemini, Microsoft Copilot. You do not need a paid subscription; free versions work perfectly.
What do I receive after purchasing?+
Immediately after purchase you receive an email with a link to your Notion document (no Notion account required) and a PDF download. Both contain all prompts organised by category.
Is this a subscription?+
No. Every Veltrix pack is a one-time purchase. Pay once, own it forever. No subscriptions, no hidden fees, no recurring charges.
What if it doesn't work for me?+
Every pack comes with a 30-day money-back guarantee. If you're not satisfied, email us and we'll refund you in full — no questions asked.
